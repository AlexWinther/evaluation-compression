### What did you struggle with?
- None of the AT methods outperform uniform random sampling on the dev run. Kossen and PPAT are within noise of uniform, and ASE is just worse
- The surrogate diagnostics show that the cause is the surrogate, not a bug in the estimator implementations
    - The loss is very concentrated. 5% of the images hold 81% of the loss, mostly positive cases that the model misses with high confidence
    - A proposal proportional to the true loss would be ~425x more efficient than uniform, so the potential for AT is large
    - The current random forest surrogates do not find these images. Spearman correlation between the Kossen score and the true loss is 0.06, giving an expected efficiency of ~1.05x
    - Training the forest on extra pool labels does not help (~1.0x), so the frozen ImageNet features seem to be the limit
    - ASE starts with a +75% bias, because its estimate depends entirely on the surrogate
    - The PPAT proxy never predicts a positive and its loss has a correlation of ~0.1 with the true loss
- The papers use much stronger surrogates (calibrated deep ensembles of the evaluated architecture, or CLIP-based models). The PPAT paper itself reports that uniform beats LURE when the surrogate does not track the loss
- My comparison is also weak. Only 20 repetitions, budgets up to 100% of the pool, and a different metric than the papers (MAPE instead of median squared error)
